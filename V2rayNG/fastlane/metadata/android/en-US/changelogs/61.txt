Fixed the "empty server list" loop: a failed refresh used to lock retries out for 15 minutes and left you staring at "No data available" — now a failed attempt is retried the very next time you open (or return to) the app, the pool also refreshes when the app comes back to the foreground (not only on a cold start), and a clear message tells you when the refresh cannot reach the pool. Also removed an unused install permission that made Play Protect more suspicious of the APK.
