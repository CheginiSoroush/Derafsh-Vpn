Derafsh 1 now refreshes the moment you open the app. Until now the built-in pool only updated on a 4-hour background timer, which battery savers on many phones (especially Xiaomi/Samsung) silently delayed for hours or days — so opening the app kept showing the same stale servers. Each launch now pulls the active source pool once (throttled to one attempt every 15 minutes) and swaps in fresh configs automatically, no manual update needed.
