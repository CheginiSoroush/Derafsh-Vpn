Smart pool management: each source is now split into groups of at most 200 configs — when a source carries more than 200, extra groups are created automatically as "Derafsh N (2)", "Derafsh N (3)", …; a single network request still fetches everything and the previous source's servers are fully replaced. Additionally, right after every real-delay test pass, configs that failed the test are removed automatically from the Derafsh groups, and chunk groups left completely empty are dissolved; configs that have not been tested yet are always kept, and your personal subscriptions are never touched.
