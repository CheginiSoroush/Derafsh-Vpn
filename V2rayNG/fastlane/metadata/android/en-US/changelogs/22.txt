Derafsh free-source switcher: a new button on the main screen opens the built-in pool of free config feeds, listed under neutral names "Derafsh 1" through "Derafsh 21" (no upstream URL is ever shown). Only the active source is auto-updated, every 4 hours; picking another source repoints the built-in subscription at it and refreshes immediately, replacing the previous pool's servers in place. Your choice is persisted across restarts and your personal subscriptions are left untouched. All 21 sources were validated live before shipping (HTTP 200 + parseable configs), expanding the pool by 6 more verified feeds over the previous test build.
