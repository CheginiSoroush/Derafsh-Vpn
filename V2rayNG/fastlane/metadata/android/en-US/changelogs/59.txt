Two user-reported fixes. Activation: codes issued by the bot are 27 characters (DRF-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX), but the input field silently cut them at 24 — the last group never made it in and every activation failed. The field now accepts the full code. Subscriptions: since the last release the built-in config pools could come up empty, because the server-side update gate rejected the pool fetches that carried no app-version declaration — the app now declares its version on every pool fetch, so Derafsh 1 and the other sources update again.
