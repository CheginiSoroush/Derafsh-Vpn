Why Derafsh

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

Instant free connection

A public free source is built right in. Open the app and it refreshes itself, delay-tests every config and buries the dead ones — no setup, no hunting.

Xray core inside

VLESS, VMess, Trojan and Shadowsocks — plus Hysteria2, with Reality, XTLS, WebSocket and gRPC transports for the toughest networks.

Privacy by design

No account, no sign-up, no tracking. The tunnel is built 100% on your device by the local Xray core — we operate no servers in your path.

A ~25 MB arm64 APK, per-ABI builds and a tuned startup profile. Runs smoothly on Android 7.0 and up.

Privacy

What Derafsh never collects

Your identity

No account, name, email or phone number. Derafsh has no sign-up — nothing to leak.

Your traffic

Processed entirely on your device by the local Xray core. No Derafsh server ever touches your connection.

Your history

No logs of visited sites, no browsing history, no precise location. Your phone keeps your stats — locally.

FAQ

Quick answers

Is Derafsh really free?

Yes. Every feature is free and the whole app is open source under GPL-3.0. A small number of ads cover the costs.

Do I need an account or an invitation code?

No. There is no sign-up, no code, no invite. Install, open, connect.

Where do the configs come from?

A public free source ships built-in and is refreshed and delay-tested automatically when you open the app. You can also add your own subscriptions or configs at any time.

Which APK should I install?

Modern phones (2016 and later): arm64-v8a. Older devices: armeabi-v7a. Emulators and Chromebooks: x86_64. When in doubt, pick universal.

Can I check the code myself?

Absolutely — the full source is public on GitHub under GPL-3.0, and every release APK is signed with the same official key.
